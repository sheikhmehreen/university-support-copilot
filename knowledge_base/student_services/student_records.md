# Student Records

## Department
Student Services

## Document Type
Student support knowledge article

## Purpose
Help students understand routine student-record requests while protecting record integrity and identity-sensitive information.

## Overview
Student records may include enrollment information, personal details, academic status, and other institutional records. The Copilot can explain documented request and correction procedures but cannot directly modify official records unless an authorized system action explicitly permits it.

Record corrections should be treated as verification workflows, not simple text edits.

## Common Questions

### I found an error in my student record.
Identify the affected record and use the approved correction process. Do not assume that a requested change is automatically accepted.

### Can the Copilot change my record?
Not unless an authorized workflow explicitly provides that capability. In a support conversation, route record-change requests to Student Services when verification or staff action is required.

### I need a copy of my student information.
Explain the documented request route. The Copilot should not disclose private record information merely because a student asks for it in chat unless the system provides an authorized, verified access workflow.

### My record has not updated after a change.
Capture the request/reference number and expected change, then route for verification. Do not claim that an update has been completed without authoritative confirmation.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Identify the type of record involved.
2. Determine whether the request is viewing information, correcting information, or asking about status.
3. Use the documented request process.
4. For corrections, collect the minimum information needed to identify the issue.
5. Preserve any case/reference number.
6. Route identity-sensitive, disputed, or staff-authorized changes to Student Services.
7. Never fabricate or manually infer official record values.

## Requirements
Depending on the workflow, support may need:
- request/reference number;
- record category;
- description of the suspected error;
- approximate date of the request;
- authorized verification information through the official channel.

Do not request unnecessary identity documents or sensitive information in an open chat.

## Troubleshooting

**Requested change is not visible:** verify status using the official system/reference and escalate if unclear.

**Student disputes a record:** do not decide which version is correct. Route for official verification.

**Identity cannot be established:** do not disclose or change the record. Use the authorized identity-verification process.

**Record correction affects another service:** explain that downstream updates may require system processing, but do not invent a processing time.

## Important Rules
Official records are authoritative only when obtained through an authorized source. The Copilot must not create, alter, delete, or “correct” records based solely on a student's statement.

Privacy-sensitive requests should use the minimum necessary information and the university's approved verification channel.

## When to Escalate
Escalate:
- record corrections;
- disputed information;
- identity-sensitive requests;
- missing updates;
- requests for official record disclosure;
- cases requiring staff authorization.

## Information to Collect
Record type, issue description, request/reference number, and minimal context needed for routing.
