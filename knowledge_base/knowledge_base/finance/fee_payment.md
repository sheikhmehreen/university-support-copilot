# Fee Payment

> Demo University is a fictional university created for this portfolio project. All values below are demo data.

## Common Questions

### How do I pay my fees?
Three methods:
1. **Online card payment** in the portal under Finance > Pay Fees.
2. **Bank transfer** using the fee voucher (download from Finance > Fee Voucher; it contains the voucher number).
3. **Bank branch deposit** with the printed voucher.

### How long until my payment appears?
Online card: within 1 business day. Bank transfer or branch deposit: within 2 business days. Receipts appear in Finance > Receipts.

### I paid but my balance still shows unpaid.
Wait 3 business days. If it still shows unpaid, email Finance with the transaction reference, date, amount and payment method. Do not pay again.

### My payment failed but money was deducted.
Do not retry. Send the transaction reference and a screenshot to Finance. Finance verifies within 5 business days; amounts deducted for failed payments are reversed by the bank or refunded.

### I was charged twice.
Send both transaction references to Finance. Duplicate charges are verified within 5 business days. See Refunds for how the overpayment is returned.

### What should I never share?
Never send your card number, CVV, PIN or banking password to anyone, including support.

## Escalate to a human when
- payment is missing after 3 business days;
- money was deducted but the payment failed;
- a duplicate charge or disputed transaction is reported.
