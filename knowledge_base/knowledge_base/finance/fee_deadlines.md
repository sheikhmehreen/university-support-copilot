# Fee Deadlines

> Demo University is a fictional university created for this portfolio project. All values below are demo data.

## Fall 2026 Fee Schedule
| Item | Deadline |
|---|---|
| First installment (60% of tuition) | 30 Sep 2026 |
| Second installment (40% of tuition) | 15 Nov 2026 |
| Late payment grace period | 1 Oct - 10 Oct 2026 |

## Common Questions

### When is my Fall 2026 fee due?
The first installment (60%) was due 30 Sep 2026 and the second (40%) is due 15 Nov 2026. Students may also pay the full amount by 30 Sep 2026.

### What is the late fee?
Rs 2,000, applied to payments made 1-10 Oct 2026.

### What happens after 10 Oct 2026 if I have not paid?
A registration hold is placed on the account: no new enrollment, no certificates, and no transcript requests until the dues are cleared.

### Can I get an extension?
Extensions are not automatic. Submit a request to Finance with your student ID and reason before the deadline. Finance decides case by case.

### The portal shows a different deadline.
Treat the Finance Office as the authority. Email finance@demo-university.example with a screenshot.

## Escalate to a human when
- the student asks for an extension, waiver or hold removal;
- two sources show different deadlines;
- the question is about a term other than Fall 2026.
