# Exams

> Demo University is a fictional university created for this portfolio project. All values below are demo data.

## Key Dates (Fall 2026)
- Midterm exams: 26 Oct - 30 Oct 2026
- Final exams: 14 Dec - 22 Dec 2026
- The exam timetable is published in the portal under **Academics > Exam Schedule** 14 days before each exam period.

## Common Questions

### I cannot access my online exam.
Immediately submit the **Exam Incident Form** (portal > Academics > Exam Incident) and email it-support@demo-university.example with the course, exact error and time. Incidents must be reported within 2 hours of the exam start.

### I submitted my exam but I am not sure it went through.
A successful submission shows a reference number starting with SUB- and sends a receipt email. If you have neither, do not resubmit. Report it as an incident right away.

### I missed an exam because of a technical problem.
Report it within 2 hours via the Exam Incident Form. Makeup requests need evidence (screenshot, error text) and must be sent to Academic Affairs within 3 business days. Approval is decided by Academic Affairs and is not guaranteed.

### How do I appeal a grade?
Submit a grade appeal to Academic Affairs within 7 days of the result being published.

## Escalate to a human when
- a live exam cannot be accessed or submitted;
- a makeup, extension or accommodation is requested;
- there is a grading dispute or an academic-integrity concern.

## Contact
