# Academic Policies

> Demo University is a fictional university created for this portfolio project. All values below are demo data.

## Common Questions

### What is the attendance requirement?
Minimum 75% attendance per course to be eligible for the final exam. Below 75% the student is marked "Not Eligible" unless Academic Affairs approves a medical or emergency exception.

### What is the grading scale?
A: 85-100 (4.0), B: 70-84 (3.0), C: 60-69 (2.0), D: 50-59 (1.0), F: below 50 (0.0).

### What is academic probation?
A cumulative GPA below 2.0 at the end of a term places the student on academic probation for the next term. Two consecutive probation terms are reviewed by the Academic Committee.

### How do I request an exception to a policy?
Email Academic Affairs with your student ID, the policy, the reason and any evidence. Requests are reviewed within 5 business days. Submitting a request does not mean it is approved.

### How do I appeal an academic decision?
Grade appeals must be submitted within 7 days of results. Other decisions can be raised with Academic Affairs within 14 days.

### What is the withdrawal deadline?
20 Nov 2026 for Fall 2026. A "W" grade appears on the transcript.

## Escalate to a human when
- an exception, appeal or probation review is requested;
- a student disputes an academic decision;
- the question is not covered by these policies.
