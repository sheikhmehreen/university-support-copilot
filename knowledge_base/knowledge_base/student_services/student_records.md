# Student Records

> Demo University is a fictional university created for this portfolio project. All values below are demo data.

## Common Questions

### Which details can I update myself?
Phone number, address and personal email can be updated in the portal under **Profile > Contact Details**. Changes apply immediately.

### How do I correct my name or date of birth?
Submit a **Record Correction Request** (portal > Student Services > Record Correction) with a scanned copy of an official ID document (CNIC or B-Form). Review takes 5 business days.

### How do I know the correction was accepted?
You receive an email with the decision and the request number (starting with RC-). Check status in Student Services > My Requests.

### Can support share or change my record in chat?
No. Records are changed only through the correction request after verification. Identity documents must be uploaded through the portal, never sent in chat.

### My record did not update after the correction was approved.
Allow 2 business days for the update to reach all systems. After that, email Student Services with the RC- number.

## Escalate to a human when
- the student disputes a record;
- identity cannot be verified;
- an approved change is not visible after 2 business days.
