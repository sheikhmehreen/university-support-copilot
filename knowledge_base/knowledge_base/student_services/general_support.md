# General Student Support and Routing

> Demo University is a fictional university created for this portfolio project. All values below are demo data.

## Which department handles what?
| Problem | Department | Email |
|---|---|---|
| Login, password, portal, technical errors | IT Support | it-support@demo-university.example |
| Enrollment, exams, grades, academic policies | Academic Affairs | academics@demo-university.example |
| Fees, payments, deadlines, refunds | Finance | finance@demo-university.example |
| Certificates, student records, general requests | Student Services | studentservices@demo-university.example |

All offices: Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00 PKT, response within 1 business day.

## Common Questions

### I do not know which department to contact.
Choose by the problem in the table above. If a technical error blocks another action (for example a payment page error), contact IT Support first and mention the other department.

### My problem involves more than one department.
Send one request to the department that owns the decision, and mention the other in the description.

### What is the expected response time?
Within 1 business day. Urgent exam-access issues are handled the same business day.

### Can staff or the Copilot approve exceptions?
The Copilot cannot. Exceptions are decided by the owning department.

## Escalate to a human when
- the request does not fit any department above;
- the student needs an official decision or approval;
- the answer is not in the knowledge base.
