# Fee Deadlines

## Department
Finance

## Document Type
Student support knowledge article

## Purpose
Handle fee-deadline questions without inventing dates or applying a deadline to the wrong student population.

## Overview
Fee deadlines may vary by academic term, program, student category, payment type, or other institutional rules. This knowledge article deliberately does not contain real deadlines.

The Copilot must state a deadline only when the current authoritative knowledge source explicitly identifies the date and its scope. A deadline mentioned by a student should not automatically be treated as verified.

## Common Questions

### When is my fee due?
If a current, authoritative deadline is available for the student's applicable context, provide it with the relevant scope. Otherwise, explain that Finance must confirm the current deadline.

### I missed the deadline. Can I still pay?
The Copilot may explain any documented late-payment process. It must not promise an extension, waiver, reinstatement, or exception.

### The deadline I was given is different from what the portal shows.
Treat the discrepancy as a verification issue. Do not select one date based on intuition. Capture the sources or references and route to Finance.

### Does the same deadline apply to everyone?
Do not assume so. Check the scope of the documented deadline, including term or student category when specified.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Identify the academic term and fee type.
2. Determine whether the student is asking for a published deadline or an exception.
3. Check the current authoritative knowledge source.
4. State the deadline only if it is explicitly documented and applicable.
5. If no verified deadline is available, say so.
6. For missed deadlines, disputes, or exception requests, route to Finance.
7. Do not invent dates to make an answer appear complete.

## Requirements
Useful context includes:
- academic term;
- fee type;
- student category/program when relevant;
- date shown in the student's official portal, if supplied;
- existing Finance reference number.

## Troubleshooting

**Portal and document show different dates:** escalate for authoritative confirmation.

**Student cannot find the deadline:** provide the approved source location if documented; otherwise route to Finance.

**Deadline has passed:** explain the documented next step if one exists, without promising approval.

**Student asks for a deadline not represented in the knowledge base:** clearly mark the information as unavailable rather than guessing.

## Important Rules
Dates are high-risk knowledge because they change. The Copilot must not generate a plausible deadline from historical patterns, academic calendars, or general assumptions.

A deadline answer should identify its applicable context whenever that context is available.

## When to Escalate
Escalate:
- missed deadlines;
- deadline exceptions;
- conflicting dates;
- account-specific deadline questions;
- requests for fee waivers or extensions;
- any deadline not verified in the current knowledge source.

## Information to Collect
Term, fee type, relevant student category, displayed date, and reference information are generally sufficient for triage.
