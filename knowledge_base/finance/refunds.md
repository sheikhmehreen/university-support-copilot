# Refunds

> Demo University is a fictional university created for this portfolio project. All values below are demo data.

## Refund Rules (Fall 2026)
| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| Duplicate payment or overpayment | 100% of the extra amount |
| Withdrawal on or before 22 Sep 2026 | 80% of tuition |
| Withdrawal after 22 Sep 2026 | No tuition refund |

## Common Questions

### How do I request a refund?
Portal > Finance > Refund Request. Provide the transaction reference, amount and reason. Duplicate/overpayment requests must be made within 14 days of the payment.

### How long does a refund take?
Up to 10 business days after approval. It is paid back to the original payment method.

### Can I get a refund after the semester has started?
Only for duplicate payments or overpayments, or withdrawal on or before 22 Sep 2026 (80%). Later withdrawals are not refunded.

### Does submitting a request mean I will be refunded?
No. Statuses are: Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Issued. Only Approved or Issued means money will be or has been sent. Review takes up to 5 business days.

### My refund was approved but I have not received it.
Wait the full 10 business days from approval. After that, email Finance with the refund reference.

## Escalate to a human when
- eligibility is unclear or disputed;
- the refund is overdue (more than 10 business days after approval);
- the original payment is disputed.
