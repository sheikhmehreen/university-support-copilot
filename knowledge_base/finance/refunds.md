# Refunds

## Department
Finance

## Document Type
Student support knowledge article

## Purpose
Explain the general refund-request workflow while preventing the Copilot from promising eligibility, amounts, or processing outcomes.

## Overview
Refunds may depend on the original transaction, payment status, applicable institutional rules, cancellation or withdrawal circumstances, and Finance verification. This article does not establish actual refund eligibility rules, processing times, minimum amounts, or payment-method-specific policies.

The Copilot can help a student understand what information is normally needed and how to request review. It cannot approve a refund.

## Common Questions

### Can I get a refund?
The Copilot should not promise eligibility. It can explain the documented criteria and request process when those criteria are available. Individual cases requiring transaction or policy review must go to Finance.

### How do I request a refund?
Use the university's approved refund-request process and provide the required reference information. If the current process is not documented, route the student to Finance rather than inventing a form or workflow.

### My refund was approved but I have not received it.
Capture the refund or transaction reference and route the case to Finance for status verification. Do not invent a processing time.

### I was charged twice. Is that a refund?
A duplicate transaction and a policy-based refund may follow different verification paths. Identify the transaction issue and route to Finance.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Identify the original payment or charge.
2. Determine whether the student is asking about eligibility, requesting a refund, or checking an existing refund.
3. Use the documented refund process when available.
4. Collect the relevant transaction or case reference.
5. Do not request sensitive financial credentials.
6. Route eligibility decisions, disputed transactions, and missing refunds to Finance.
7. Do not promise a refund amount or completion date.

## Requirements
Useful information:
- payment/transaction reference;
- payment date;
- amount;
- reason for the refund request;
- existing refund/case reference;
- current status shown by the university system, if available.

Never request full card details, PINs, banking passwords, or authentication codes.

## Troubleshooting

**Refund request cannot be submitted:** capture the error and route technical issues to the appropriate support channel.

**Refund status is unknown:** use the existing reference and ask Finance to verify status.

**Student expects an automatic refund:** explain that eligibility and processing must be confirmed under the applicable rules.

**Original payment is disputed:** Finance should verify the transaction before a refund outcome is discussed.

## Important Rules
A refund request is not the same as a refund approval. The Copilot must clearly distinguish:
- request submitted;
- request under review;
- request approved;
- refund issued;
- refund received.

Only an authoritative system should establish which status applies.

## When to Escalate
Escalate:
- eligibility questions requiring policy interpretation;
- disputed charges;
- duplicate transactions;
- missing or delayed refunds;
- transaction-verification needs;
- requests for exceptions.

## Information to Collect
Collect only transaction-safe details needed for Finance review.
