# Fee Payment

## Department
Finance

## Document Type
Student support knowledge article

## Purpose
Guide students through the general fee-payment process and support safe triage of payment confirmation problems.

## Overview
Students may need help understanding supported payment methods, payment status, missing confirmations, failed transactions, duplicate charges, or receipt information. The Copilot may explain only the payment methods and workflow explicitly documented by the university.

This article intentionally does not state actual bank details, account numbers, payment gateways, fees, currency rules, or deadlines.

## Common Questions

### How do I pay my university fees?
Use the currently approved payment method described by the university. Follow the official payment instructions and retain the transaction or receipt reference generated by the payment channel.

### I paid but my student account still shows an outstanding balance.
Do not immediately instruct the student to pay again. First collect the transaction reference, payment date, amount, and payment method when available, then route the case to Finance for verification.

### My payment failed.
Record the failure message and payment channel. If the payment provider indicates that no transaction occurred, the student may retry according to the provider's guidance. If money was deducted or the status is unclear, Finance should verify the transaction before another payment is made.

### I was charged twice.
Do not assume which transaction is valid. Collect the available references and escalate the duplicate-charge case to Finance.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Identify the payment type and intended student account.
2. Determine whether the student is asking for instructions, reporting a failure, or disputing a completed payment.
3. For a normal payment question, provide the documented payment process.
4. For missing confirmation, collect safe transaction details.
5. For a failed or ambiguous transaction, avoid recommending duplicate payment until status is known.
6. For duplicate charges or disputes, route to Finance.
7. Never request a password, full card number, CVV, PIN, or authentication code.

## Requirements
Useful support information:
- transaction/reference number;
- payment date and approximate time;
- amount;
- payment method/provider;
- visible status;
- student-safe account identifier when required by the support process.

Do not collect:
- full card details;
- PIN;
- CVV/security code;
- banking password;
- one-time authentication code.

## Troubleshooting

**Payment succeeded but receipt is missing:** preserve the transaction reference and route for confirmation.

**Balance has not updated:** do not assume failure or success. Finance should reconcile the transaction.

**Payment failed before completion:** capture the error and payment channel.

**Money was deducted but payment is marked failed:** treat as an ambiguous transaction and escalate.

**Duplicate payment:** preserve both references if available and escalate rather than promising a refund.

## Important Rules
Payment status must come from an authoritative financial system or payment provider. The Copilot must not claim a transaction is settled, reversed, refunded, or rejected without evidence.

Do not provide banking instructions from memory. Use only current approved payment information.

## When to Escalate
Escalate:
- missing payment confirmation;
- deducted funds with failed/pending status;
- duplicate charges;
- disputed transactions;
- receipt discrepancies;
- account balance disputes;
- requests requiring transaction verification.

## Information to Collect
Transaction reference, date/time, amount, payment method, visible status, and relevant error are normally sufficient.
