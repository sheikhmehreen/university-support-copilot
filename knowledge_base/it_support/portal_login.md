# Portal Login

## Department
IT Support

## Topic
Portal Login

## Purpose
Provide general demo guidance for students who cannot sign in to the university support portal.

## Information
The student portal is used for common academic and student-service tasks. A login problem may be caused by incorrect credentials, an expired password, a temporary account lock, or a browser/session issue.

This is fictional demo knowledge for the University Student Support Copilot. It does not represent a real university system or policy.

## Common Questions
- I cannot sign in even though I believe my credentials are correct.
- The portal keeps returning me to the login page.
- My account appears to be locked.
- The portal works on one browser but not another.

## Procedure
1. Confirm that the student is using the current portal login page.
2. Ask the student to verify the username and retry without copying extra spaces.
3. Ask the student to try a private/incognito browser window.
4. If the issue persists, collect the error message, approximate time of failure, browser/device, and whether other portal pages are accessible.
5. Route persistent account-access problems to IT Support.

## Escalation
Escalate when the account appears locked, the student receives a persistent authentication error, or troubleshooting does not resolve the issue. Do not request or store the student's password.
