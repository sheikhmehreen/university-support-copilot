# Portal Login

## Department
IT Support

## Document Type
Student support knowledge article

## Purpose
Help the Student Support Copilot guide students through common university portal sign-in problems without requesting credentials or making unsupported account decisions.

## Overview
The student portal is the normal entry point for supported academic, financial, and student-service actions. Login failures can result from an incorrect portal address, an incorrect username, an expired password, a locked account, a stale browser session, or a service-side problem.

This knowledge article is intentionally generic. It does not define a real university URL, username format, password policy, lockout duration, or service-level agreement. Those values must come from an approved university configuration or current operational documentation before the Copilot states them as facts.

## Common Questions

### I cannot log in. What should I check?
First confirm that you are using the university's current official portal address. Then verify that the username is entered correctly and retry the sign-in. If the portal reports a specific error, preserve the exact wording because it helps IT Support determine the failure type.

### My password is correct but the portal rejects it.
Do not repeatedly guess the password. Use the approved password-reset process if the account may have an expired or unknown password. If reset is unavailable or does not resolve the problem, route the case to IT Support.

### The portal worked earlier but does not work now.
A temporary session, browser, network, or service problem may be responsible. Retry once in a private/incognito window or another supported browser. If multiple users appear affected, treat the issue as potentially service-wide and escalate.

### The portal says my account is locked.
The Copilot must not promise an automatic unlock time or manually declare an account unlocked. Follow the approved account-recovery process or escalate to IT Support.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Confirm the student is attempting to access the correct university portal.
2. Ask what happens after the student submits the login form.
3. Capture the exact error message when one is displayed.
4. Ask the student to verify the username without sharing a password.
5. If appropriate, suggest retrying in a private/incognito browser window.
6. If the problem persists, suggest another supported browser or device when practical.
7. If the student suspects an expired or forgotten password, direct them to the password-reset procedure.
8. If the account appears locked, recovery fails, or the portal appears unavailable, escalate to IT Support.
9. Never ask the student to send a password, authentication code, recovery secret, or full credential set through support chat.

## Requirements
Useful troubleshooting information includes:
- portal or service being accessed;
- exact error message;
- approximate time of failure;
- browser and device;
- whether another browser was tested;
- whether the problem occurs repeatedly;
- whether the student can access other university services.

Do not collect information that is unnecessary for troubleshooting.

## Troubleshooting

**Blank page or endless loading:** retry once, use a private window, and try another supported browser. Persistent failure should be escalated.

**Invalid credentials message:** verify the username and use the approved reset process if the password may be expired or forgotten.

**Session or redirect error:** close the affected browser session, reopen the portal, and retry. Persistent redirect problems belong with IT Support.

**Works on one device but not another:** compare browser/device behavior and record the affected environment.

**Several services fail simultaneously:** do not assume the student's account is the cause. Escalate as a possible service or identity-platform incident.

## Important Rules
The Copilot may provide troubleshooting guidance, but it must not view, store, or request passwords. It must not claim that an account is unlocked, reset, suspended, or restored unless that status is supplied by an authoritative system. It must not invent a portal URL or authentication policy.

## When to Escalate
Escalate when:
- authentication continues to fail after basic troubleshooting;
- account lock or identity verification is suspected;
- password recovery does not work;
- the portal appears unavailable to multiple users;
- the student cannot complete a time-sensitive university action;
- the issue requires an account change or staff authorization.

## Information to Collect
Collect only the minimum diagnostic information required: service, error, time, browser/device, and relevant troubleshooting already attempted.
