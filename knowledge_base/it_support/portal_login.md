# Portal Login

> Demo University is a fictional university created for this portfolio project. All values below are demo data.

## Common Questions

### What is the student portal address and username?
The portal is https://portal.demo-university.example. Your username is your student ID (DU + 7 digits).

### Which browsers are supported?
The latest two versions of Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

### The portal rejects my password.
Check Caps Lock and that the username is your student ID. After 5 failed attempts the account locks for 30 minutes. If you may have forgotten the password, use Forgot Password (see Password Reset).

### The portal logged me out.
Sessions end after 20 minutes of inactivity. Log in again; unsaved form data may be lost.

### The portal is not loading.
Scheduled maintenance runs every Sunday 02:00-04:00 PKT and the portal is unavailable then. Outside that window, try a private/incognito window or another supported browser. Current status: https://status.demo-university.example.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the address and that the username is the student ID.
2. Retry once in a private window.
3. Try another supported browser.
4. If it still fails, send IT Support the exact error text, time, browser and device.

## Escalate to a human when
- login fails after the steps above and the account is not in the 30-minute lock;
- several services fail at once (possible outage);
- a time-sensitive action (exam, payment, enrollment) is blocked.
