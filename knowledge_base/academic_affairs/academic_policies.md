# Academic Policies

## Department
Academic Affairs

## Document Type
Student support knowledge article

## Purpose
Provide grounded general information about academic policy questions without turning the Copilot into an unauthorized decision-maker.

## Overview
Academic policies can govern enrollment, attendance, assessments, progression, grading, academic standing, and exceptions. The Copilot should summarize only policies present in the approved knowledge base or authoritative connected source.

An individual student's circumstances can require interpretation, evidence review, or staff discretion. In those situations, the Copilot should explain the general rule and route the case instead of presenting an interpretation as a binding decision.

## Common Questions

### Can the university make an exception to a policy?
The Copilot should not answer yes or no unless the current policy explicitly defines the authority and process. It should explain the documented exception process when available and route the request to the authorized department.

### What is the deadline for an academic request?
State a deadline only when the current knowledge source explicitly provides it and the applicable term/context is clear. Otherwise, advise the student to confirm the current deadline with Academic Affairs.

### Does this policy apply to my situation?
The Copilot may explain the general policy and identify relevant conditions. It should not make a final determination when individual records or discretionary judgment are involved.

### I disagree with an academic decision.
Do not argue the merits or invent an appeal outcome. Explain the documented review or appeal route if available and escalate when the process is not documented.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Identify the policy topic and desired outcome.
2. Determine whether the student needs general information or an individual decision.
3. Retrieve the relevant approved policy content.
4. State only what the source supports.
5. Distinguish explicit policy requirements from general guidance.
6. Identify any missing information that prevents a reliable answer.
7. Route individual exceptions, disputes, or authorization requests to Academic Affairs.

## Requirements
For policy triage, useful information may include:
- policy topic;
- academic term;
- program or course context when relevant;
- type of request;
- relevant deadline or event date if supplied by the student;
- existing case/reference number.

Do not ask for unrelated personal information.

## Troubleshooting

**Policy is not in the knowledge base:** say that the available knowledge source does not establish the answer and route for confirmation.

**Two policy statements appear to conflict:** do not choose one based on intuition. Identify the conflict and escalate.

**Student asks for a special exception:** explain the documented exception mechanism, if available, and route the request.

**Student provides a screenshot of a policy:** use it as user-provided context but do not silently treat it as current institutional policy if authoritative confirmation is required.

## Important Rules
Never invent policy language, exceptions, approval criteria, deadlines, penalties, or appeals outcomes. Avoid categorical statements when the source is incomplete.

The Copilot should use calibrated language such as “the available policy states…” and “Academic Affairs should confirm…” when individual interpretation is required.

## When to Escalate
Escalate:
- policy exceptions;
- disputed academic decisions;
- conflicting policy sources;
- individual eligibility determinations;
- appeals;
- requests requiring staff authorization;
- cases where the current policy cannot be verified.

## Information to Collect
Collect topic, context, requested outcome, applicable term, and existing reference information when relevant.
