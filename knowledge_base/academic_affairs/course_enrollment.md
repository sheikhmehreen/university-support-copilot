# Course Enrollment

> Demo University is a fictional university created for this portfolio project. All values below are demo data.

## Key Dates (Fall 2026)
- Enrollment window: 1 Sep - 15 Sep 2026
- Add/Drop period: 16 Sep - 22 Sep 2026
- Withdrawal deadline (with "W" grade): 20 Nov 2026

## Common Questions

### How do I enroll in a course?
Log in to the portal, go to **Academics > Course Registration**, search the course code, and click **Enroll**. A confirmation message and email are sent.

### How many credit hours can I take?
Minimum 9 and maximum 18 credit hours per term. Going above 18 needs approval from Academic Affairs.

### The course is full.
Join the waitlist (up to 10 students per section). If a seat opens, you get an email and have 24 hours to confirm.

### The system says I do not meet the prerequisite.
Prerequisites are enforced by the system. To request an exception, email Academic Affairs with your student ID, the course code and your reason. Exceptions are reviewed within 3 business days.

### I enrolled but I do not see the course.
Wait 15 minutes and refresh. Check your email for the confirmation. Do not enroll twice. If there is no confirmation, contact Academic Affairs.

### Can I drop a course?
Yes during Add/Drop (16-22 Sep) with no record. After that, withdrawal until 20 Nov 2026 shows a "W" grade.

## Escalate to a human when
- a prerequisite or credit-limit exception is requested;
- the enrollment record looks wrong;
- the enrollment page is down during the window (also tell IT Support).
