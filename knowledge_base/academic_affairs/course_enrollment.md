# Course Enrollment

## Department
Academic Affairs

## Document Type
Student support knowledge article

## Purpose
Help students understand the general enrollment workflow and distinguish routine enrollment questions from cases requiring Academic Affairs review.

## Overview
Course enrollment can depend on the academic term, enrollment period, course availability, prerequisites, student status, program requirements, and other rules maintained by the university. This document intentionally does not state real course codes, dates, capacity numbers, prerequisite rules, or exception policies.

The Copilot may explain the documented process but must not approve enrollment, promise a seat, override a prerequisite, or decide that an exception should be granted.

## Common Questions

### How do I enroll in a course?
Use the university's approved enrollment interface during the applicable enrollment period. Search for the course, review the available course information, and submit the enrollment action. The exact interface and workflow should come from current university documentation.

### The course is full.
A full course should not be represented as available merely because a student needs it. The Copilot should explain any documented waitlist or alternative process. If no such process is documented, escalate to Academic Affairs.

### The system says I do not meet a prerequisite.
Do not advise the student to bypass the restriction. Explain that prerequisite exceptions require the appropriate academic authority and route the case when an exception may be warranted.

### I enrolled but the course is not visible.
Verify the transaction or enrollment status using the authoritative student system. If the status is inconsistent or unclear, escalate rather than assuming the enrollment succeeded.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Identify the academic term and course.
2. Determine whether the student is asking about normal enrollment, a technical failure, or an exception.
3. If the issue is technical, collect the exact message and route appropriately.
4. If the system identifies a prerequisite, capacity, eligibility, or enrollment-period restriction, explain that the restriction is controlled by the academic system.
5. Direct the student to the documented enrollment workflow.
6. For exceptions, disputed records, or approval requests, route to Academic Affairs.
7. Never state that an exception is approved unless an authoritative source confirms it.

## Requirements
For support triage, useful information may include:
- term;
- course name/code;
- exact error;
- approximate time;
- whether the course appeared available;
- whether the student submitted the enrollment action;
- any reference or confirmation generated by the system.

Do not ask for passwords or unnecessary academic records.

## Troubleshooting

**Enrollment button unavailable:** check whether enrollment is open and whether the course has a documented restriction. Do not invent an opening time.

**Prerequisite error:** explain that the system is applying a rule and route exception requests.

**Course full/closed:** follow the documented waitlist or department process if one exists.

**Enrollment submitted but status is unclear:** avoid repeated submissions because duplicate actions may create conflicting requests. Verify status first.

**Technical error:** capture exact error, browser/device, and time; route to IT Support when appropriate.

## Important Rules
Enrollment decisions belong to authorized academic systems and staff. The Copilot must not override prerequisites, create seats, guarantee enrollment, or interpret an individual exception as approved.

The Copilot must not invent deadlines. If a deadline is not explicitly present in the current knowledge source, it should say that the exact deadline needs confirmation.

## When to Escalate
Escalate:
- prerequisite exceptions;
- capacity or closed-course disputes;
- enrollment-period disputes;
- disputed enrollment records;
- program-specific eligibility questions not covered by the knowledge base;
- requests for academic approval;
- unresolved system failures.

## Information to Collect
Collect term, course, issue type, error message, relevant reference number, and steps already attempted.
